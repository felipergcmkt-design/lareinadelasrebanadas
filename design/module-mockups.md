# Mockups de los módulos

## Actualización de la tercera imagen

A petición del usuario, la tercera imagen muestra una tabla de costos y gráficos de barras y de distribución, a partir de datos de simulación de la hoja «Costos y precios». Se conserva la etiqueta de simulación en la página. El arte de referencia se trazó con tablas y gráficos antes de aplicarlo al mockup.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit / product-mockup.
Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: a photorealistic open cream-paper ebook mockup on a pale blush tabletop. Image 2 is the exact REPLACEMENT PAGE ARTWORK, with a spreadsheet-style cost table on the left and a bar chart plus donut chart on the right.
Change ONLY the interior printed page artwork of the open book in Image 1 to the supplied two-page financial spread in Image 2. Map the exact left page cost table and the exact right page charts naturally onto the curved paper. Keep the table, graph types, axes, chart proportions, category labels and simulation labeling from Image 2. Do not invent data, numerical claims or new diagrams.
Preserve Image 1's camera position, spine geometry, natural page curl, cream matte paper, realistic paper edges, binding, contact shadow, warm soft lighting, pale pink tabletop, pencil, and generous empty upper 40% for a CSS website overlay. Keep the single book completely inside the frame, centered across the lower half, with the same margin and perspective. Vertical 4:5 output.
The pages must look naturally printed, photographic and crisp. This is a website product illustration, not a spreadsheet screenshot. No added overlay text outside the pages, no people, no new props, no baked-in gradient or pink overlay, no watermark, no flat pasted screen. Palette remains cream #FFF8F0, berry #A92350, pale rose and a restrained gold accent.
```


Tres mockups ilustrativos generados con la herramienta de imágenes integrada, basados en páginas de los ebooks del programa. Los originales permanecen en su ubicación de generación. Las versiones WebP de 960 y 480 píxeles de ancho se usan en la página de ventas con carga diferida y un degradado CSS.

## 1. bases

- Imagen: `assets/modulo-01-recetas-libro.webp`
- Variante móvil: `assets/modulo-01-recetas-libro-480.webp`
- Referencia: Recetario, páginas 12–13.

### Prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup.
Create an ultra photorealistic premium editorial product photograph of one REALISTIC OPEN PRINTED BOOK as a mockup of a digital ebook. Use the supplied flat two-page spread as the exact visual reference for the two visible pages: keep the printed subject, photographs, cream paper and berry-pink editorial layout recognizable and map them naturally onto gently curved pages. The reference is source page artwork, not the desired final composition.
Composition: vertical 4:5 image. A single open book sits across the LOWER HALF, centered, completely inside frame with generous 7% side margins and 7% bottom margin. Camera at a natural 40-degree oblique angle from above, angled only slightly, showing the entire two-page spread and realistic spine, subtle paper curl, edge thickness and soft contact shadows. Book pages must be visible and look photographed, not a flat pasted rectangle.
The upper 40% of the image is smooth empty pale blush/cream tabletop, free of objects and text, reserved for a website overlay. The book should fill roughly 85% of the width and the lower 48% of the composition. No furniture or room scene.
Style: natural high-end cookbook photography, warm diffused window light, matte cream paper, tiny authentic paper grain, crisp detailed food photographs printed on the pages, restrained palette #FFF8F0 cream, #A92350 berry pink, pale rose and a subtle warm brown accent. NO baked-in pink overlay or gradient: it will be added by the website. No headline outside the book, no floating objects, no hands, no screens outside the book, no watermark, no sales badge, no mockup frame, no cartoon, no glossy plastic, no flowers, no new price or revenue claim. Keep exactly one open book with two facing pages.
Theme 1: recipes and ingredient preparation. The LEFT page shows the actual tres leches rectangular layered cake and slice photo from the reference, with the heading Tres leches. The RIGHT page shows the ingredient-list layout from the supplied reference with its berry-pink section bars. A very small whisk and a folded dusty pink kitchen cloth can sit near the lower outer edge without hiding any page. The main object is the open recipe book.
```
